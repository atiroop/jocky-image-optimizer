# Roadmap

1. **Milestone 1 — Plugin Foundation (current):** bootstrap, settings, diagnostics, tests/CI, docs, and ZIP packaging.
2. **Milestone 2 — Image Processing Engine:** safe JPEG/PNG optimization adapters, atomic output validation, and unit tests.
3. **Milestone 3 — Next-generation Formats:** WebP/AVIF derivatives and safe WordPress delivery integration.
4. **Milestone 4 — Automatic and Bulk Optimization:** post-upload pipeline, bounded queue, CLI worker, retries, pause/resume, and recovery.
5. **Milestone 5 — Backup and Restoration:** managed originals, restoration workflow, and interrupted-operation recovery.
6. **Milestone 6 — Cloudflare R2:** optional adapter after studying existing offload workflows and compatibility.
7. **Milestone 7 — Production Hardening:** security review, load and compatibility testing, operational docs, and release packaging.

Do not claim parity with ShortPixel: encoders and settings produce different quality and file-size results.
